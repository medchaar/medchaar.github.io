Profile

Engineering leadership, delivered with rigour

A project manager who pairs deep process-engineering knowledge with disciplined governance.

Senior Project Manager with over 12 years of experience delivering turnkey dairy and food projects. Proven record of managing multimillion-dollar installations from concept to commissioning, applying PMBOK principles, ensuring GMP and Food Safety compliance while driving technical innovation to enhance performance and sustainability.

Currently at Synlait Milk Limited, managing a portfolio of 15+ capital and improvement projects valued at over NZ$10M — delivering business-critical upgrades in process capability, hygienic design, reliability and regulatory compliance across Synlait's manufacturing operations.

Previously with Tetra Pak (New Zealand & Tunisia) for over a decade, leading materials handling and wet-process projects across New Zealand, Australia, France and North Africa — from powder handling and CIP/SIP to aseptic technology and packaging.

Career grounded in an Automation & Industrial Engineering honours degree and early experience as a Control & Instrumentation Engineer in Oil & Gas (British Gas, ENI, SONATRACH, GNPOC).

Career in Depth

Extended experience

For recruiters who'd like the full story — my earlier project delivery at Tetra Pak Tunisia and my foundational Oil & Gas control & instrumentation career.

Project & Automation Engineer — Wet Process & Materials Handling
Tetra Pak · Tunisia & North Africa
2012 – 2016

Delivered turnkey processing and packaging projects across the Maghreb dairy & beverage market — from pre-project engineering and quotation through to design, automation, commissioning and hand-over. Acted as the technical bridge between customers, Tetra Pak specialists and sub-suppliers.

  • Led UHT line projects for leading dairy producers including Soummam and Hodna Lait — covering flavoured milk and chocolate/biscuit beverages, from process design to on-site commissioning.
  • Delivered a cold cream-dessert plant for GIPA and aseptic storage & distribution tanks for CLM (Centrale Laitière), integrating mixing, thermal treatment and CIP/SIP.
  • Produced pre-project engineering, process solution design and functional descriptions, and prepared technical quotations and budgets for major FMCG customers — Nestlé, Danone, Coca-Cola and Cevital.
  • Managed project budgets, procurement and progress via SAP, coordinating cross-functional teams and sub-contractors to keep scope, cost and schedule on track.
  • Owned FAT/SAT, commissioning, performance testing and operator training — ensuring GMP and food-safety compliance at start-up.

Control & Instrumentation Engineer — Oil & Gas
EMCO · British Gas Tunisia · EPPM
2009 – 2012

Foundational career delivering instrumentation, control and safety systems for upstream and midstream Oil & Gas facilities across North Africa — spanning design, integration, factory acceptance testing and field commissioning for major international operators.

  • Engineered and commissioned ICSS / DCS & ESD systems for operators including ENI, Sonatrach-Agip, Mellitah Oil & Gas, GNPOC and Arabian Gulf Oil Company.
  • Worked hands-on with control & safety platforms such as HIMA (safety PLC/ESD), Yokogawa and ABB System 800xA — configuration, logic and I/O integration.
  • Executed FAT at vendor facilities and SAT / field commissioning on wellheads, gas-processing and metering skids, including loop checks and instrument calibration.
  • Prepared instrumentation datasheets, loop diagrams, cause-&-effect matrices and commissioning documentation to international O&G standards.
  • Coordinated with EPC contractors and client teams to close punch-lists and achieve safe, on-schedule start-ups in demanding field environments.
